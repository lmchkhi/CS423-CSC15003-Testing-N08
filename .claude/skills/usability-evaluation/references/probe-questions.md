# Probe questions (post-session)

HW03 requires, at minimum, open-ended probes covering clarity, error
recovery, speed, and trust. Ask these *after* the SUS/UEQ-S scale, and adapt
the bracketed part to what you actually observed in that specific session —
generic probes asked the same way every time produce shallow answers.

- **Clarity** — "Was there any point where you weren't sure what to do next?
  [reference the moment you observed hesitation, if any]"
- **Error recovery** — "You [ran into X / corrected Y] at one point — walk me
  through what you were thinking there." (skip gracefully if the participant
  hit zero errors, and note that in the session file)
- **Speed** — "Did the flow feel faster or slower than you expected for a
  task like this? Which step felt like it took the longest?"
- **Trust** — "At [checkout / payment / account step], did you feel
  confident the site was handling your information/order correctly? Why or
  why not?"
- Flow-specific wrap-up — "Is there anything about [the specific flow] that
  would stop you from using this site for real?"

Keep these conversational, not a read-aloud script — the goal is to dig into
the *specific* difficulties observed in that session, not to collect the same
five answers seven times.
